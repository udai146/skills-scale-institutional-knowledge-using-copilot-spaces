# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads keep cross-functional work moving from planning through release by coordinating dependencies, surfacing blockers, and helping the team maintain momentum.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate sprint or milestone execution across teams and functions
- Remove or escalate blockers that slow delivery
- Align delivery plans with product priorities and engineering capacity
- Monitor progress against release and dependency milestones
- Partner with the Project Manager to keep schedules, risks, and stakeholder expectations in sync

### Goals
- Maintain predictable execution and reduce delivery friction
- Improve handoffs between planning, implementation, and release stages
- Support team focus on the highest-priority work

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and milestone reviews
- Dependency and risk check-ins with PM and engineering leads
- Status updates for leadership and partner teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on schedule health and dependencies
- Supports Product Managers by translating priorities into execution readiness
- Coordinates with Developers and QA to reduce bottlenecks and keep work flowing

---

## QA / Quality Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Quality Leads define the quality strategy for delivery and ensure that features meet acceptance criteria, release expectations, and customer impact requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, regression coverage, and release quality gates
- Validate that features meet acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Partner with Developers on testability and defect triage
- Help assess release readiness and risk for production deployments
- Support traceability between stakeholder expectations and verified outcomes

### Goals
- Reduce defects and regressions before release
- Improve confidence in the quality of delivered work
- Ensure quality is visible, measurable, and consistent across milestones

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage with engineering and product partners
- Quality updates during sprint and milestone check-ins

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review test plans and root-cause analysis
- Collaborates with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria and business validation
- Coordinates with Project Managers and Delivery Leads on release timing and risk assessment

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Partners help teams manage security, privacy, and governance requirements so projects can move forward without exposing avoidable risk.

### Responsibilities
- Review projects for security, privacy, compliance, and operational risk
- Define or confirm secure development and review practices
- Support incident readiness, vulnerability triage, and remediation planning
- Partner with engineering and stakeholders on governance and control requirements
- Help assess risk trade-offs during planning, execution, and release

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Strengthen trust and operational resilience
- Ensure required controls are embedded early rather than added at the end

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews and compliance checkpoints
- Security assessments during planning and release windows
- Escalation communication during incidents or high-risk findings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to integrate secure design and validation practices
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on risk, mitigation, and release constraints
- Collaborates with Delivery Leads and QA to support readiness and secure rollout

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Partners

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Stakeholder Partners represent user and business needs, ensure stakeholder voices inform prioritization, and help prepare the organization for adoption and rollout.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, adoption concerns, and business context with the project team
- Support stakeholder communication and update cadence
- Help validate readiness for launch, adoption, and change management
- Identify risks, dependencies, or support needs that affect release success
- Partner with Product and Project leads on stakeholder alignment

### Goals
- Improve adoption readiness and customer impact
- Keep stakeholders informed, aligned, and engaged
- Ensure business outcomes are considered throughout delivery

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates and milestone briefings
- Feedback loops with product, delivery, and support teams
- Adoption readiness reviews before and after launch

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides customer context to Product Managers for prioritization and success metrics
- Keeps Project Managers and Delivery Leads informed of stakeholder dependencies and adoption concerns
- Helps Developers and QA understand real-world use cases and expected outcomes

---

## Engineering Managers / Tech Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Tech Leads guide technical execution, team health, architecture decisions, and the practical delivery of high-quality software.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical direction, architecture trade-offs, and engineering quality standards
- Support planning, staffing, and execution across the engineering team
- Help balance delivery speed with maintainability, technical debt, and reliability
- Partner with Delivery Leads to manage capacity and resolve implementation blockers
- Support risk identification and mitigation for technical dependencies and design decisions

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound solutions that meet product and business goals
- Improve team effectiveness and sustainable delivery pace
- Reduce avoidable technical risk and rework

### Typical Communication
- Technical planning and design reviews
- Engineering health updates and dependency discussions
- Escalation paths for architecture, staffing, or delivery risks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate goals into feasible technical strategies
- Supports Project Managers and Delivery Leads on sequencing, capacity, and milestone realism
- Guides Developers and QA on engineering standards, testability, and release confidence

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles make ownership, communication, escalation, and delivery quality clearer across cross-functional project work.
